NeurIPS 2026 · Poster

Neural Structural Reasoner: A Brain-inspired Architecture for Reasoning over Structured Knowledge

Zixing Jia*, Yuhang Pan*, Ni Ji* Equal contribution

We built a network that represents a knowledge graph in the connections between neural populations. Its connections learn through local Hebbian updates, without backpropagation. Following the sequence of active neurons reveals how the network arrives at a prediction.

NeurIPS 2026 · 海报论文

Neural Structural Reasoner: A Brain-inspired Architecture for Reasoning over Structured Knowledge

Zixing Jia*, Yuhang Pan*, Ni Ji* 共同第一作者

我们构建了一个类脑推理模型，将知识图谱中的实体及关系编码在神经元群体及其连接中。模型通过局部赫布规则学习，无需反向传播。沿着神经元的激活顺序，可以追踪模型得出预测的中间步骤。
